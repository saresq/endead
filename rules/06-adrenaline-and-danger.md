## Adrenaline Points

*p.15*

> Tell me how you kill zombies, and I'll tell you who you are.
> — Elle

For each Zombie eliminated, a Survivor gains 1 Adrenaline Point (AP) and moves up a notch on their Danger Bar. Some game features provide additional AP, like taking Objective tokens or consuming food.

## Danger Levels

*p.15*

There are 4 Danger Levels on the Danger Bar: Blue, Yellow, Orange, and Red. Reaching a new Danger Level provides the Survivor with a new Skill (P. 63) to help them on their Mission. Skills stack across Danger Levels. As new Skills are unlocked, place new trackers on the Survivor Dashboard to indicate them.

- A Survivor starts the game at **Blue** Level with 0 Adrenaline Points and a single Skill.
- The **Yellow** Level is reached with 7 Adrenaline Points. The Survivor gains a fourth Action.
- The **Orange** Danger Level is reached with 19 Adrenaline Points. Choose a Skill among the 2 indicated at this Level.
- The **Red** Danger Level is reached with 43 Adrenaline Points. Choose a Skill among the 3 indicated at this Level.

| Danger Level | AP threshold | Skill gained | Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | 0 | 1 starting Skill (fixed) | 3 |
| Yellow | 7 | +1 Action | 4 |
| Orange | 19 | choose 1 of 2 | 4 |
| Red | 43 | choose 1 of 3 | 4 |

> The table restates the four paragraphs above; the AP numbers, the fourth Action, and the 1-of-2 / 1-of-3 choices are all printed on p.15.

## Global Danger Level

*p.15*

Building up Adrenaline has a side effect. When players draw a Zombie card to spawn Zombies, read the line that corresponds to the highest Danger Level achieved by any Survivor (see Zombie Spawn, P. 25). The stronger the Survivor is, the more Zombies appear.

*Diagram captions, p.15:*

- Blue Level: 1 Walker
- Yellow Level: 2 Walkers
- Orange Level: 4 Walkers
- Red Level: 6 Walkers

## Skill acquisition

*p.15 and p.63*

Skills stack across Danger Levels. From the Skills chapter (P. 63):

> The effects of the following Skills and/or bonuses are immediate and may be used during the Turn in which they are acquired. This means that if an Action causes a Survivor to level up and gain a Skill, that Skill may be used immediately if the Survivor has any Actions remaining (or the Survivor may use any extra Actions the Skill grants).

> Each Survivor in Zombicide has specific Skills with effects described in this section. In case of a conflict with the general rules, the Skill rules have priority.

> **Not in the rulebook** — which Skills each Survivor is offered at each Level is printed on the ID Cards, not in the book. See [15-characters.md](15-characters.md#core-box-survivors).
